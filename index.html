Our story

A kitchen built around one fire

Chef Marco Bellini grew up cooking in his grandmother's village kitchen on the Ligurian coast. At Olive & Ember, everything passes through our wood-fired oven: bread baked each morning, vegetables from local farms, and fish landed the same day.
